BrainGym

Full-Stack Interactive Cognitive Assessment & Brain Training Platform

About The Platform

BrainGym is a lightweight, high-performance web platform designed to evaluate and train core cognitive functions through gamified, interactive mental exercises. Built with a responsive dark-neon UI, it provides users with scientific performance metrics, tracking accuracy and reaction times down to the millisecond.

The Problem

In today's fast-paced digital world, maintaining mental agility is becoming increasingly difficult. People are facing:

  • Cognitive Decline: Prolonged screen time and passive media consumption lead to reduced attention spans and mental fatigue.
  • Lack of Accessible Benchmarking: Clinical brain-training software is often locked behind paywalls or requires bulky installations.
  • No Transparent Analytics: Casual games fail to provide scientific data like hit/miss ratios or millisecond latency tracking.

The Solution

BrainGym solves these issues by providing a free, data-driven, and highly accessible training environment.

Users can instantly log on, select their difficulty tier, and undergo rigorous mental conditioning. Every session concludes with an advanced analytics board that displays personal accuracy, speed, and direct comparisons to average human baseline benchmarks.

The Training Modules

Our platform features five distinct, scientifically-inspired assessment modules:

Reflexes

1. Reaction Time Test

Measures pure neuromuscular reflex latency by tracking how quickly you respond to a sudden visual color shift in milliseconds.

Focus

2. Stroop Test

Evaluates cognitive flexibility and selective attention by forcing your brain to process conflicting visual colors and words.

Recall

3. Memory Match

Tests short-term visual recall. Memorize a progressively expanding sequence of random digits before they vanish.

Speed

4. Mental Math Sprint

Challenges quantitative processing speed and arithmetic agility under strict, customizable countdown timers.

Memory

5. N-Back Test

The ultimate working memory challenge. Monitor a rapid stream of letters and flag matches from 'N' steps back.
